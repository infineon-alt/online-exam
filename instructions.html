Assessment Instructions

Duration: 60 Minutes

Total Questions: 30

Total Marks: 30

Instructions

  • Read each question carefully before selecting your answer.
  • The assessment consists of 30 questions.
  • The total duration of the assessment is 60 minutes.
  • Each question carries 1 mark.
  • There is no negative marking unless specified in the question.
  • Use the Next and Previous buttons to navigate between questions.
  • You can use the Question Palette to directly navigate to any question.
  • You may change your selected answer before submitting the assessment.
  • Keep your camera enabled and ensure your face remains visible throughout the assessment.
  • Do not refresh, close or navigate away from the assessment window.
  • Switching browser tabs or windows may be recorded.
  • The use of mobile phones, external websites, AI tools or any other unauthorized resources is strictly prohibited.
  • AI-generated, copied or plagiarized responses may be detected through assessment-integrity checks. Any violation may be reported and may result in immediate disqualification from the assessment.
  • Make sure you have a stable internet connection before starting.
  • The assessment will be submitted automatically when the timer reaches 00:00.
  • Once the assessment is submitted, you cannot change your answers.
